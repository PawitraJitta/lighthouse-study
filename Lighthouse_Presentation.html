Thai Grower Segments
Three segments. Different trust systems.
Credit · Behavior · LINE · Influence pathway
👴
Somchai, 63
Senior Landowner · Rice
~3.2M farmers · 40% of market
"I've farmed 40 years. Whatever the shop says, I buy."
💳 Credit: BAAC loan every pre-season. Buys inputs ON credit at retailer — spending decision is already made before harvest revenue arrives
🌿 Spray behavior: Seeing is Believing
"Spray by Feeling" — reacts when disease appears, calls retailer for product. No MOA awareness · No resistance rotation
💬 LINE behavior: LINE = family chat only. Zero agri content consumption. Trust lives in face-to-face at the shop — not digital
🎯 Reach via: RETAILER FIRST — transform the advisor, not the farmer
🧑‍🌾
Prayong, 47
Commercial Core · Multi-crop
~2.8M farmers · 35% of market
"If it cuts costs, I'll try it — but show me proof first."
💳 Credit: Uses BAAC + retailer credit tab. Tracks costs per rai, compares prices online before going to shop — but still buys in-store
🚁 Drone: Hires drone every season for spraying. Trusts drone operator's product recommendations more than reading a label
💬 LINE behavior: Active in village LINE groups and Facebook farmer communities. Shares prices, disease alerts. Trust = peer proof + trial results
🎯 Reach via: DRONE OPERATORS + LINE groups + field trial content
🚁
Nat, 28
Agri-Entrepreneur · Drone Op
~1.2M farmers · 15% · fast growing
"I track everything on my phone. I need data, not advice."
💳 Credit: Less BAAC-dependent, uses commercial credit. Plans per-season ROI. Considers drone service income in crop investment decisions
📊 Decision style: Data-driven — tracks yield, input cost, MOA rotation consciously. Open to new products if trial data supports it
💬 LINE behavior: Follows agri LINE OA, watches YouTube demos. Shares content with 50–200 farmers he services — a digital multiplier
🎯 Reach via: DIRECT digital + co-create content — he's the influencer

Thailand Lighthouse · 2026
Pain Point A
The Digital Paradox
📱 Smartphone penetration 91%
🌾 Retailer-dependent purchasing decisions 80%+
Farmers have the device.
They don't have the trust.
94.7% of Thai farmers use LINE daily — but LINE = family/community chat, NOT product discovery. The trust context is personal, not commercial. Campaigns that land in LINE without a trust bridge get ignored.
139% mobile penetration + 85.4% internet — digital reach is technically easy. The barrier is not connectivity. It's that farmers trust people they've known for 20 years, not apps or brand content. Any system that bypasses this trust chain fails.
100% of Syngenta's farmer apps reach only non-first touch (those already aware). The rural-urban digital gap is closing fast — 46.3% of Thais are on LINE, Facebook reaches even state-rural accounts. The challenge is not reach. It is relevance and trust.

Strategic Response · Pain Point A
Retailers are not the only gatekeepers.
And that gap is a strategic opportunity.
👥
Fellow Farmers
"If my neighbor's crop looks better, I ask what they used."
Peer-to-peer trust is the oldest and most powerful influence channel in Thai agriculture. Already active — just not digitally connected yet.
Digital opportunity: LINE group amplification
🎙️
KOLs — Key Opinion Leaders
"He's been farming for 30 years. If he says it works, I believe it."
Respected local voices with agronomic credibility. Their endorsement carries more weight than brand advertising — especially for Senior and Commercial Core segments.
Digital opportunity: content co-creation + testimonial
Highest Priority
🚁
Drone Operators
"They spray my neighbor's field too. I can see the difference myself."
Younger generation · High digital adoption · Visit 10–50 farms per week · See crop results firsthand · Trusted as technical experts. They are a mobile proof point — they make "Seeing is Believing" happen at scale, farm by farm.
Digital opportunity: LINE OA program + product certification
👁️
"Seeing is Believing" is the dominant purchase logic in Thai agriculture.
Syngenta already has strong ad engagement signals. The next step is to activate peer influence channels digitally — especially drone operators — to scale "Seeing is Believing" beyond the retailer touchpoint. Thailand has the right audience segments already engaged. We just need to route influence through the right channels.

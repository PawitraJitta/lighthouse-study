Pain Point A
The Digital Paradox
📱 Smartphone penetration 91%
🌾 Retailer-dependent purchasing decisions 80%+
Farmers have the device.
They don't have the trust.
94.7% of Thai farmers use LINE daily — but LINE = family/community chat, NOT product discovery. The trust context is personal, not commercial. Campaigns that land in LINE without a trust bridge get ignored.
139% mobile penetration + 85.4% internet — digital reach is technically easy. The barrier is not connectivity. It's that farmers trust people they've known for 20 years, not apps or brand content. Any system that bypasses this trust chain fails.
100% of Syngenta's farmer apps reach only non-first touch (those already aware). The rural-urban digital gap is closing fast — 46.3% of Thais are on LINE, Facebook reaches even state-rural accounts. The challenge is not reach. It is relevance and trust.

Pain Point B
Retailer Controls
the Last Mile

❌ No digital touchpoint here
Farmer

80%+ of crop protection purchase decisions are made AT the retailer, not by the farmer.
Retailers recommend by margin — not by MOA. Syngenta has no digital presence in this moment.
⚠️ Retailer Tax Barrier
Agri-retailers — especially small and mid local shops — operate largely in the informal economy. Any digital system that captures their transaction data creates a tax exposure they fear. This is not resistance to technology. It is rational economic self-protection. Any system that forces digital tracking on them will be rejected.
💸 The Credit & Pricing Lock
The retailer who provides credit to the grower controls what the grower buys, at what price, and from whom. As long as the retailer is the grower's bank, the retailer controls the commercial outcome. No digital tool can overcome this until the credit dependency is addressed. LINE CRM's price-lock feature is designed to address exactly this.
